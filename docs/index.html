Method and limits

  • PM2.5 comes from the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service global analysis, which combines satellite observations with an atmospheric model on a grid of about 40 km. It is not a ground measurement and understates roadside peaks.
  • Weather comes from the ERA5 reanalysis for training and from the Open-Meteo forecast for the live prediction.
  • Model: gradient boosted trees predicting the change in log PM2.5 from today to tomorrow, using recent pollution levels, today's and tomorrow's weather, season and day of week.
  • The range shown with each forecast is an 80 percent interval taken from past forecast errors. It covered 77.5 percent of outcomes in the test year.
  • Today's value includes Copernicus forecast hours for the part of the day that has not happened yet.
